Vision

What I want to learn next.

I used to hear a deadline in the question of who I would become. These days, I can look ahead and see room to try something.

Follow a measurement into the software

I like the moment in a lab when a reading makes me go back to the setup. Was the assumption wrong? Did I measure the thing I meant to measure? There’s something satisfying about being able to try again and see what changes.

Studying Industrial Engineering with a focus on Electrical Engineering gives me several ways into that question. I’d like more practice following a signal all the way from a machine to the person who has to decide what to do with it. I’m curious about where uncertainty gets lost along the way.

Give people a way back into the decision

One small question in ORZEN keeps my attention. If a commitment moves and the day no longer fits, which decision can the planner reasonably make for me? There’s a very human difference between being relieved that something moved and opening the calendar to find that something important has disappeared.

In AI Agents Studio, the same interest takes a different form. I can watch an agent walk to a workstation before a job begins. That gives me a place to look when work appears to stall. I’d like to keep making software whose choices I can follow, question and change.

Leave space for the people doing the work

At the end of a camp weekend, a kitchen still needed cleaning. Some of the volunteers who had finished their own jobs came back to help. When I think about how I’d like to work with other people, I think about that kitchen more often than I think about the schedule we made.

I hope to work with people who tell each other when a plan has become too much, who make it easy to ask for a second opinion, and who have enough room to try an idea of their own. I’m learning how to make that room for others and how to take it when someone offers it to me.

The weekend in Herbstein

A future I can stay curious about

I don’t have a timetable for any of this. There are technical skills I want to earn through practice, conversations I hope will change my mind, and things I’ll probably spend a weekend building just to see whether they work. I want enough space in my life for all three.

If one of these questions is something you’re working through too, I’d enjoy hearing where it has taken you.
