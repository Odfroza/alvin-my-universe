Vision

What I want to learn next.

I’d like my next engineering role to bring me closer to the point where a program meets a real machine, a real schedule, or a person with a decision to make.

Back to About

Follow a measurement into the software

Studying Industrial Engineering with a focus on Electrical Engineering lets me explore hardware, measurements, and software while also considering how technical decisions affect the wider system. I want more practice following signals into software and understanding where uncertainty enters.

Make a decision understandable

In ORZEN I’m exploring when a system can move something on its own and when it should ask. In Studio I’m exploring how to show that a job is waiting, travelling to a workstation, or running. I want to keep testing those ideas with small cases I can actually inspect.

Keep space for people

I’d like to work with people who can explain a constraint early and allow someone else to improve the plan. That is a lesson I want to bring forward from working with volunteers.
