Chapter 04

Building a Life

In Kiel, studying Industrial Engineering with a focus on Electrical Engineering has helped me follow questions from an idea into measurements, technical systems, and decisions.

At the lab bench

At HAW Kiel, a lab usually ends with a report. Before I can write it, I have to decide what to measure, take the readings, and work out what the numbers let me say. I like that last part. Sometimes the most interesting line in the report is the one that explains why I need another measurement.
