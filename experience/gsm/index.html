The small details behind every invoice

I worked with service billing (Leistungsabrechnung) at GSM GmbH’s central finance office in Kiel from July 2024 to October 2025. The work involved checking digital information against attendance documentation and preparing records used for invoicing to public employment agencies.

I learned that a process can look straightforward from the outside, but become much harder when information changes between reporting periods or a single entry does not match its supporting documents.

Making the recurring work easier to follow

I built small Excel tools for my own workflow. They helped carry forward prior-month information, compare participant records across months, flag differences and upcoming deadlines, and organize data for Excel-to-Word invoice preparation.

These were practical workflow aids, not a claim that I replaced the accounting system or independently automated the whole department. The goal was to make repeated checks easier to inspect.

What I took from it

This experience changed how I think about reliability. A useful system is not only one that can calculate an answer; it should also help a person notice when the information behind that answer deserves another look.
