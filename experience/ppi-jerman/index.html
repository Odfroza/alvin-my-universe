Starting with a conversation

A national organization sees a list of branches. Each branch sees the people who turn up, the room they can book, and the time someone has between classes. In my department, we used one-on-one conversations to hear more of that local picture, particularly from branches we heard from less often.

Keeping local ownership

For Temu PPI Cabang, I helped coordinate and mentor regional teams in the Berlin, Hamburg, and Frankfurt representation areas. Each region had its own lead. We worked on choosing a learning topic and making room for discussion that made sense to the people coming.

A different kind of responsibility

LDK 5.0 gave me a much more direct operating role. I chaired the volunteer committee. The transport change, the way we divided work, and the public record of the weekend are in the case study below.
