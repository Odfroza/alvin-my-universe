I spent years trying to become someone responsible and capable. In 2026 I finally stopped to ask whose picture of adulthood I was working toward. I still like building things and being there for people. I’m getting more curious about which parts of that life I would choose for myself.

01 / How I Think

A strange result or a decision I don’t understand can keep me asking questions for hours. I like tracing what led to it. With people, I’m learning to leave room for an answer I wouldn’t have arrived at myself. Sometimes the best thing I can do with a question is listen.

02 / What I Value

Being someone a friend can count on matters to me. It might mean fixing a light, making time for a conversation, or keeping a promise. I’m learning to let the care go both ways. Asking for help gives someone else the same chance to show up for me.

03 / What Drives Me

ORZEN began with a frustration I knew from my own day. In AI Agents Studio, I wanted to see what happened between giving a task to a system and getting an answer. A question I can actually try out will keep me at my desk for a long time.

04 / Beyond the Work

I love a conversation that starts with something ridiculous and somehow lasts two hours. I forget things, get carried away with ideas, and like spending time with people who can laugh about that with me. Those ordinary days are the ones I hope I remember.

There’s plenty I still want to figure out. For once, that feels like something to look forward to.
