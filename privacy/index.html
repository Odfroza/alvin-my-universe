Visiting

The site loads its own fonts, images and scripts. It adds no analytics, tracking cookies or advertising. Reading position and portrait controls stay in the current page. When visited online, the hosting provider handles ordinary connection information.

Sending a message

When you choose Send message, FormSubmit processes your name, email and message for forwarding to my inbox. I use those details to read your note and reply. Please send only what you are comfortable sharing. FormSubmit says it can hold submissions for up to 30 days, including while a recipient is awaiting activation.
