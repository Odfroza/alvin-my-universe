Reading the combinations

Four patterns.

The report gives names to pairs of factor scores. I use these as prompts for reflection. A questionnaire is one snapshot of how I answered that day.

Pattern 8.1

Persistent

High conscientiousness · Low neuroticism

When an initial plan runs into difficulty, this pairing suggests a preference for steady, organised follow-through.

Pattern 10.1

Clear-Thinking

Low neuroticism · High openness

This combination pairs composure with interest in unfamiliar ideas. It describes a tendency to pause and consider another explanation when a problem gets complicated.

Pattern 3.1

Socially Self-Confident

High extroversion · Low neuroticism

This pairing describes someone who may feel comfortable speaking up, approaching people and taking initiative in a group.

Pattern 9.1

Enlightened Traditionalist

High conscientiousness · High openness

An interest in new approaches can coexist with an appreciation for structure and continuity. This pattern describes exploring what could change while keeping what already works.
